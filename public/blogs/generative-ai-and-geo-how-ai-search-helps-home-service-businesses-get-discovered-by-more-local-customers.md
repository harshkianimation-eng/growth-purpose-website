## 3. Essential JSON-LD Schema Markup for Contractor AI Recognition

Embedding structured JSON-LD Schema code into your home service website's header is the most effective technical step to help AI engines comprehend your business entity architecture.

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Electrician",
  "name": "The Growth Purpose Electrical - Sector 56 Gurgaon",
  "url": "https://thegrowthpurpose.com/locations/gurgaon-sec-56",
  "logo": "https://thegrowthpurpose.com/logo.png",
  "image": "https://thegrowthpurpose.com/images/electrical-van.jpg",
  "telephone": "+919876543210",
  "areaServed": {
    "@type": "AdministrativeArea",
    "name": "Gurgaon Sector 56"
  },
  "address": {
    "@type": "PostalAddress",
    "streetAddress": "Plot 123, Sector 56",
    "addressLocality": "Gurgaon",
    "addressRegion": "Haryana",
    "postalCode": "122011",
    "addressCountry": "IN"
  },
  "geo": {
    "@type": "GeoCoordinates",
    "latitude": 28.4211,
    "longitude": 77.0825
  },
  "openingHoursSpecification": {
    "@type": "OpeningHoursSpecification",
    "dayOfWeek": ["Monday", "Tuesday", "Wednesday", "Thursday", "Friday", "Saturday", "Sunday"],
    "opens": "00:00",
    "closes": "23:59"
  }
}
```

### 3 Rules of Contractor Schema Optimization
1. **Differentiate Trade Categories:** Use explicit trade schemas (`Plumber`, `Electrician`, `HVACBusiness`, `HousePainter`, `PestControlService`) matching your core services.
2. **Include Verified Service Area Coordinates:** Explicitly state exact cities, postal codes, and GPS latitude/longitude coordinates within your Schema markup.
3. **Validate Code Frequently:** Use Google's Rich Results Test and Schema Validator to ensure zero syntax errors exist in your JSON-LD code.

Traditional SEO focuses on ranking for specific keywords in 10 blue search links or map pins. AI search synthesizes information across multiple web sources, reviews, and Schema data to provide 1 direct, conversational recommendation answering complex home service prompts.

JSON-LD Schema is machine-readable code embedded in your website that explicitly defines your business name, service area boundaries, phone number, operating hours, trade categories (`Plumber`, `Electrician`, `HVACBusiness`), and licensing proof, allowing AI crawlers to comprehend your business entity accurately.

### What is Entity Authority in contractor AI search optimization?
Entity Authority refers to how clearly and reliably AI models understand your home service business as a legitimate, trustworthy local contractor based on your website data, owner profiles, social media activity, and customer reviews across the web.
